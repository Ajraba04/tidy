# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers using constrained Macs, particularly 8 GiB machines, who need enough disk and workload headroom to continue building without losing useful caches, projects or local data. They need quick, low-overhead inspection and explicit control over changes.

## Product Purpose

Tidy is a native macOS developer headroom utility. It inventories resource use, explains what is known and unknown, protects the current working set and presents narrow, reviewable actions. Success means the configured headroom target is met with truthful results; it never guarantees build success or extra physical RAM.

## Brand Personality

Plain, cautious, precise. These qualities follow the approved product contract, not a new branding direction. Explain evidence and consequences without health scores, exaggerated savings or urgency marketing.

## Anti-references

No generic one-click optimizer, fake RAM booster, automatic force-quitter or broad destructive cleaner. No decorative dashboard that consumes the resources it is meant to preserve. No hidden effects behind friendly category names.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the existing native SwiftUI vocabulary and familiar macOS controls.
2. Show the exact scope, consequence, selection and eligibility of every action.
3. Make comprehensive controls discoverable with conservative defaults and progressive disclosure.
4. Preserve uncertainty: unavailable, blocked, partial and failed are not success or zero.
5. Bound work and keep the interface responsive on low-spec hardware.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The approved plan requires keyboard navigation, VoiceOver labels, text scaling, contrast and accessible empty/error/denied states. Respect native appearance, Reduce Motion and user settings. State must have text/icon meaning, not color alone.
